01

Overview

Activewear fabric is specified by how it moves, not just what it is. The buyer decisions are: stretch direction (2-way versus 4-way), recovery (does it hold shape after a workout?), surface (brushed buttery versus smooth technical), and wicking (how fast does it move moisture?). Get those four right and the program works; get them wrong and the garment fails in the first wash.

Our sportswear library covers yoga, swimwear, general activewear and functional stretch, across 150–280 GSM. Every record specifies fibre, stretch system, surface and application with evidence status. Performance claims — moisture wicking, UPF, chlorine resistance, compression — are treated as testable properties with methods agreed to your specification, never as a construction name doing the talking.

05

Sourcing

Sportswear sourcing runs from our Shanghai office to mills with tricot and functional-finishing capability, then into Bangladesh through Uttara. Elastane supplier and knit construction are named requirements in the mill shortlist.

  • Stretch-system-first matching: elastane %, knit, mechanical finish.
  • Wicking/UPF/chlorine resistance tested to agreed methods.
  • Inline inspection at the mill, final verification before shipment.
  • MOQ, lead time and port terms confirmed per construction and source.
06

Quality gates

Every program in this category runs the two documented gates — inline inspection at the source mill and pre-shipment verification — against acceptance criteria you approve, with the findings log and verification report delivered to you and your factory.

07

Frequently asked questions

What is 4-way stretch?

Fabric that stretches significantly both crosswise and lengthwise (2-way stretches mainly crosswise). For yoga and swim programs, 4-way with high recovery is the standard specification.

How do you verify moisture wicking?

By agreed test method — typically a wicking height or dry-time protocol (e.g. AATM 1979 reference) — against a target you approve in the order specification.

Do you source UPF-rated swim fabric?

Yes. UPF performance is a testable property; we specify the target (commonly UPF 50) and the reference method, and provide results against it.

Can you match a hand-feel reference?

Yes — send a sample. We match fibre, knit, mechanical finish and brushing level to the reference and confirm the match with strike-offs.

What are typical MOQs for activewear fabric?

MOQ varies by construction, colour and source mill and is confirmed per option we return. Multi-colour programs are quoted together for better minimums.
