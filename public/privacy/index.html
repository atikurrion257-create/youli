Privacy Policy

What we collect when you send a brief, why we collect it, how long we keep it, and how you can reach us about it.

Request a Fabric MatchBrowse the Fabric Library

This policy is a launch draft for legal review. The registered entity name, data-processing details and retention schedule are confirmed against the company evidence register before final publication. Last reviewed: 10 September 2026 (launch draft).

01

1. Who we are

YOU LI CHINA WORLD BANGLADESH (尤利中制品有限公司) operates this website to support business enquiries for fabric, yarn and performance-material sourcing. The contact details on the Contact page are the means to reach the data controller. The exact registered entity details are published once confirmed in the company evidence register.

02

2. What we collect

We collect only what the business workflow needs: from RFQ, sample and contact forms — your name, work email, company or organization (where provided), country, the enquiry details you submit, and any files you attach (tech packs, reference images, specification sheets). We also record technical data needed to operate the site securely: the page you submitted from, the timestamp, and basic connection information for fraud and spam prevention. We do not collect sensitive personal data, and we do not use tracking profiles for advertising.

03

3. Why we collect it

Enquiry data is used to route and answer your request: matching the brief to the responsible team (Dhaka or Shanghai), preparing samples, quotations and documentation, and recording the program in the order file. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in responding to a business enquiry you initiated, and — where we process data under a contract or pre-contract steps — that contractual relationship. File uploads are processed solely to prepare and document your enquiry.

04

4. How long we keep it

Enquiry and order-file data are kept for the life of the relationship plus the period required for warranty, documentation and legal obligations; enquiries that do not become programs are retained for a limited period for support and security purposes and then deleted or anonymized. The retention schedule is finalized with the data-processing review before publication.

05

5. Who we share it with

Enquiry data may be shared within the two offices that operate the service (Dhaka and Shanghai) and, strictly for the purpose of your enquiry, with the source mill or supplier that is the subject of the brief — for example, the specification you ask us to sample. We do not sell personal data. We do not display client names or program details on this website without written permission.

06

6. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the processing of your personal data, and to object to or port it. To exercise any of these, use the contact details on the Contact page; we will respond within the period required by the applicable law. You may also withdraw consent to future communications at any time via the link in the message.

07

7. Security

The site is served over HTTPS, form delivery is validated server-side, and access to enquiry data is limited to the staff who work the workflow. No personal data is stored in analytics events; analytics on this site are limited to aggregate, non-personal usage measures.

08

8. Changes

This policy is reviewed when the service changes. Material changes are noted with a new review date at the top of this page. The date shown above is the current review date for this draft.
