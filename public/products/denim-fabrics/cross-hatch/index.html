Cross-Hatch Denim

Double-twill surface effect — a distinctive diagonal-cross texture for fashion programs.

Request a Fabric MatchBrowse the Fabric Library
01

Overview

Cross-hatch denim knits two opposite twill directions together, producing a small X-shaped cross on the surface. The effect reads as a fine diamond texture with unusual light play — a signature look for premium fashion denim that cannot be achieved with a single twill direction.

We source cross-hatch from 9 oz to 13.5 oz. The double-twill structure is denser than regular denim at the same weight, which changes drape and cost; both are specified in the order file before sampling.

Quality gates for cross-hatch denim

Every cross-hatch denim program runs the two documented gates: inline inspection at the source mill during production and pre-shipment verification before the container seals — both against the acceptance criteria you approve, both documented in the order file.

08

Questions from the denim fabrics desk

What weights do you source?

From 6.0 oz light ring-spun to 14.5 oz heavy workwear denim. The library records show the range per construction family.

Can you match a specific indigo shade?

Yes. Send a physical reference or a lab-dipped sample. We match to your reference with ΔE targets documented in the order file and verified at both quality gates.

Do you offer stretch denim?

Yes — elastane-containing denims for fitted programs. Elastane percentage and recovery are specified per order.

What is your shrinkage control?

Shrinkage is controlled through yarn (sanforised yarn options), processing and relaxation, to your stated tolerance. The tolerance is written into the specification and verified at pre-shipment.
