01

Overview

Denim is warp-faced twill — indigo warp, usually white weft — but a denim program is really three decisions in one: the weight (oz), the yarn (ring-spun versus open-end, slub versus even), and the finish (sanforised, enzyme-washed, coated). Get those three right and the garment behaves; get them wrong and no wash cycle can fix it.

Our denim library covers the five construction families that carry most apparel programs, from 6.0 oz ring-spun to 14.5 oz coated. Each record specifies weight, yarn type, width and finishing options with evidence status. Colour is managed to your indigo reference with ΔE targets in the order file, and shrinkage — the eternal denim negotiation — is controlled to your tolerance and verified at both quality gates.

02

Why this category, through YOU LI

Yarn-level matching

Denim quality is decided in the yarn. We match by yarn count, spinning and slub character, so the fabric you approve as a strike-off is the fabric that arrives in bulk.

Colour and shrinkage control

Indigo shade is managed to your reference with ΔE targets; shrinkage is controlled to tolerance through yarn and processing. Both are verified inline and before shipment.

Program-ready delivery

Rolls, labels, count and documentation arrive verified — ready for your cut-and-sew line in Bangladesh, with delivery responsibility written into the order file.

05

Sourcing

Denim sourcing runs from our Shanghai office to mills with dedicated denim spinning and dyeing, then into Bangladesh through Uttara. Indigo dyeing capability and sanforisation equipment are named requirements in our mill shortlists.

  • Yarn-first matching: count, spinning, slub character.
  • Indigo lab dips and strike-offs before bulk commitment.
  • Inline inspection at the mill, final verification before shipment.
  • MOQ, lead time and port terms confirmed per weight, colour and source.
06

Quality gates

Every program in this category runs the two documented gates — inline inspection at the source mill and pre-shipment verification — against acceptance criteria you approve, with the findings log and verification report delivered to you and your factory.

07

Frequently asked questions

What weights do you source?

From 6.0 oz light ring-spun to 14.5 oz heavy workwear denim. The library records show the range per construction family.

Can you match a specific indigo shade?

Yes. Send a physical reference or a lab-dipped sample. We match to your reference with ΔE targets documented in the order file and verified at both quality gates.

Do you offer stretch denim?

Yes — elastane-containing denims for fitted programs. Elastane percentage and recovery are specified per order.

What is your shrinkage control?

Shrinkage is controlled through yarn (sanforised yarn options), processing and relaxation, to your stated tolerance. The tolerance is written into the specification and verified at pre-shipment.

What documentation comes with bulk denim?

Roll-level weight and width records, colour reference, shrinkage data and our pre-shipment verification report. Additional test reports per your program requirements.
