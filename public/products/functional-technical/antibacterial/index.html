Antibacterial Fabric

Odour and hygiene control through functional finish or functional yarn — tested, not claimed.

Request a Fabric MatchBrowse the Fabric Library
01

Overview

Antibacterial fabric controls microbial growth through a functional finish (commonly silver-ion or zinc-ion systems) or through the yarn itself. The two systems differ in durability: finish-based performance can wash out unless the system is designed for it, while yarn-based performance is structural and permanent. The order specification always names the system and the wash-fastness target, because “antibacterial” that disappears in three washes is a claim, not a fabric.

We source antibacterial fabric from 120 GSM to 320 GSM for uniforms, hygiene programs and workwear. Efficacy and wash durability are specified against reference methods (ISO 20743 / ISO 22196 family) with targets you approve.

09

Questions from the functional & technical fabrics desk

How do you verify a performance claim?

By converting the claim into a reference test method, an acceptance target and a checkpoint in the quality plan. Results are delivered against your targets, not ours.

Are the reference methods fixed?

No — they are agreed per program. We recommend industry references (ISO, AATM, ASTM) and you approve the method and target in the order specification.

Do you carry certified FR fabric?

We source FR cotton and FR fibre constructions. Compliance to a standard (e.g. NFPA-type requirements) is documented per program with the relevant test evidence; certification scope is always stated clearly.

What does “recycled” mean in your library?

Recycled content claims follow a strict evidence process: transaction certificates and chain-of-custody documentation. Anything without the documents is marked “verification in progress” and never published as certified.
