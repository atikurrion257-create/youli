01

Overview

A functional fabric makes a promise — it resists water, fights odour, slows flame, holds strength or carries recycled content. The difference between a promise and a specification is a test method and an acceptance target. Our rule is simple: no performance claim leaves this library without a method, a target and a place in the quality plan. Where a test has not yet been run for your program, the record says so and asks for the specification instead.

This category covers the five performance families that appear most often in buyer briefs — antibacterial, waterproof, fireproof, high-strength and recycled — across 90–400 GSM. Each record names the performance system (finish, yarn or structure), the reference test methods, and the evidence status of the claim. Send us your target values and we will return mill-matched options with the test plan built in.

05

Sourcing

Functional sourcing runs from our Shanghai office to mills with the specific finishing or yarn capability required — not a general catalogue. The performance system is the first shortlist criterion, and the test plan is written before sampling.

  • System-first matching: finish, yarn or structure named in the shortlist.
  • Test plan agreed before sampling begins.
  • Inline inspection at the mill, final verification before shipment.
  • MOQ, lead time and port terms confirmed per system and source.
06

Quality gates

Every program in this category runs the two documented gates — inline inspection at the source mill and pre-shipment verification — against acceptance criteria you approve, with the findings log and verification report delivered to you and your factory.

07

Frequently asked questions

How do you verify a performance claim?

By converting the claim into a reference test method, an acceptance target and a checkpoint in the quality plan. Results are delivered against your targets, not ours.

Are the reference methods fixed?

No — they are agreed per program. We recommend industry references (ISO, AATM, ASTM) and you approve the method and target in the order specification.

Do you carry certified FR fabric?

We source FR cotton and FR fibre constructions. Compliance to a standard (e.g. NFPA-type requirements) is documented per program with the relevant test evidence; certification scope is always stated clearly.

What does “recycled” mean in your library?

Recycled content claims follow a strict evidence process: transaction certificates and chain-of-custody documentation. Anything without the documents is marked “verification in progress” and never published as certified.

Can you match a functional spec from a tech pack?

Yes. Send the tech pack or reference values; we return mill-matched options with the test plan, lead time and MOQ for each option.
