01

Overview

Knit fabric is formed in one continuous loop: the loop structure is what gives knit its stretch, drape and recovery — and what makes its specification a little more subtle. The same “T-shirt fabric” can differ by yarn type, gauge, knit course, mechanical construction and finish, which is why we specify knits by loop structure, yarn and finish as separate variables rather than a single name.

Our knit library covers the seven constructions that carry most apparel and activewear programs, from 130 GSM single jersey to 650 GSM sherpa. Every record carries composition, weight, width, hand-feel and application data with evidence status, and every program runs the same two quality gates: inline inspection at the source mill and pre-shipment verification.

05

Sourcing

Knit sourcing runs from our Shanghai office to mills across Jiangsu, Zhejiang and Guangdong, then into Bangladesh through Uttara. Mechanical construction and finish are the two variables that most often change between mills, so both are locked in the specification before sampling.

  • Construction-first matching by knit course, gauge and yarn.
  • Strike-offs and lab dips before bulk commitment.
  • Inline inspection at the mill, final verification before shipment.
  • MOQ, lead time and port terms confirmed per construction, colour and source.

Quality gates

07

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between single jersey and interlock?

Single jersey is a one-sided loop (right face, reverse loops) — lighter and cheaper. Interlock is a double-knit loop with a flat, similar face on both sides — denser, more stable and better for polos and structured garments.

Can you control shrinkage on cotton knits?

Yes — through yarn selection (ring-spun, sanforised yarn), mechanical processing and relaxation. The shrinkage tolerance is written into the order specification and verified at both quality gates.

Do you source recycled-content knits?

We source rPET polyester knits. Recycled content claims follow a strict evidence process: transaction certificates and chain-of-custody documentation before anything is published as certified. Status per order.

What width and length do you deliver in?

Widths from 30 to 72 in by construction; delivered folded or tubular per your factory requirement, with roll length and count verified at pre-shipment.

How do you handle colour consistency across lots?

Colour is managed to a light-source standard (D65 reference) with ΔE targets in the specification. Repeats are sampled against the approved master at pre-shipment.
