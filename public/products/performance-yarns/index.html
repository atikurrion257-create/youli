01

Overview

Performance usually starts in the yarn. A capillary polyester moves moisture before any finish touches the fabric; a silver-ion yarn carries antibacterial performance that no finish can wash out; an FR fibre carries flame resistance that is part of the molecule. Sourcing at yarn level gives you two advantages: durability of performance (it survives the garment’s working life) and specification honesty (the function is verifiable at the yarn, not just promised on the fabric).

This library covers the six functional yarn families that appear in our buyer briefs. Each record is treated as a capability lead until manufacturer authorisation and test data are collected for a specific program — that is stated in the evidence status rather than papered over. Send us the function you need and we will return yarn-matched options with supplier, test plan and fabric construction together.

02

Why this category, through YOU LI

Performance that survives the working life

Yarn-level function is structural. Where a finish can wash out, an ionised fibre or an FR molecule does not — and the order specification says which system applies.

Supplier transparency

Functional yarns come from named suppliers with technical dossiers. Brand-name systems (e.g. Coolmax®) require manufacturer authorisation — we track that status per record instead of implying it.

Fabric + yarn specified together

We quote the yarn and the fabric construction as one package, so the performance you specify at yarn level is the performance the finished fabric delivers.

Sourcing

Yarn sourcing runs from our Shanghai office to functional-yarn suppliers and mills across China. Supplier technical dossiers, manufacturer authorisation (where brand systems apply) and the program test plan are assembled before sampling begins.

  • Supplier-first matching: dossier and authorisation status checked before quotation.
  • Yarn and fabric specified as one package.
  • Yarn-level and fabric-level verification at both quality gates.
  • MOQ, lead time and port terms confirmed per yarn system and source.
06

Quality gates

Every program in this category runs the two documented gates — inline inspection at the source mill and pre-shipment verification — against acceptance criteria you approve, with the findings log and verification report delivered to you and your factory.

07

Frequently asked questions

Can you supply branded performance yarns like Coolmax®?

Brand-name systems require the manufacturer’s authorisation for use and claim. We track authorisation status per record and will confirm it in writing before any branded claim is used in your program.

How is yarn-level performance verified?

By the supplier technical dossier plus program testing — both at yarn level (where applicable) and at finished-fabric level, against the reference methods agreed in the order specification.

Do you source yarn for our own mills to weave?

Yes — yarn supply to a weaving or knitting step you control is within our service scope. The specification and quality plan follow the same structure.

What MOQ applies to functional yarns?

MOQ follows the supplier and the dye lot structure. We confirm it per option with the lead time and fabric construction together.

Can you match a fabric made with a specific yarn?

Send the fabric or the yarn specification; we match supplier, fibre and structure and return a strike-off for approval.
