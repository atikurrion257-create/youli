01

Overview

Woven fabrics are built on the loom: warp and weft interlaced at right angles into a structure that resists stretch and holds its shape. That makes woven construction the backbone of dress shirts, trousers, overalls, outerwear shells and uniform programs — the categories where dimensional stability, hand-feel consistency and colour fastness are negotiated before a single roll is cut.

YOU LI sources woven fabric across a defined construction map — eight structures and the four principal fibre families (cotton, TC, CVC and polyester). For each record in this library you get composition, weight, width, finish and application data, together with an evidence status for every field. Where a value is not yet documented against a specific mill lot, the library says “request specification” rather than guess. Send us a construction, a target weight, or a reference swatch and we will return mill-matched options with sampling and inspection planned from day one.

02

Why this category, through YOU LI

Mill matching, not a photo album

Each woven request is matched against mills by construction, fibre and finish capability. You receive the relationship type (direct, partner or cooperative) for every recommended source, so you know exactly what “direct mill relationship” means for your order.

Specification control

Weight, width, shrinkage, colour and construction are agreed before production, inspected inline at the mill, and re-verified before shipment. The acceptance criteria are yours to approve — not a generic promise.

Bangladesh execution

Import, customs coordination, local distribution and factory delivery are handled from our Uttara, Dhaka office, with the responsibility for each step written into your order file.

05

Sourcing

Woven sourcing runs from our Shanghai office to Chinese mills across Jiangsu, Zhejiang and the Pearl Delta, then into Bangladesh through our Uttara office. Each order file carries the mill identity type, agreed specification, inspection checkpoints and delivery responsibility.

  • Construction-first matching: we shortlist mills by weave capability, not catalogue size.
  • Strike-offs and lab dips are sampled before bulk commitment.
  • Inline inspection at the mill, final verification before shipment.
  • MOQ, lead time and port terms confirmed per construction, colour and source.
06

Quality gates

Every program in this category runs the two documented gates — inline inspection at the source mill and pre-shipment verification — against acceptance criteria you approve, with the findings log and verification report delivered to you and your factory.

07

Frequently asked questions

What is the MOQ for woven fabric?

MOQ varies by construction, colour and source mill. Yarn-dyed and multi-colour programs typically carry higher minimums than solid colors. We confirm the exact MOQ when we return your mill-matched options.

Can you match a reference swatch?

Yes. Send a physical sample or high-resolution photos with target weight and width. We return strike-offs or equivalent mill options with a written specification comparison.

Do you stock woven fabric in Bangladesh?

We coordinate local distribution from Uttara, Dhaka. Stocked items are listed in the library; made-to-order items follow the sourcing process with confirmed lead times.

Which certifications apply to woven programs?

Certification depends on the mill and the program. We provide certificate metadata — issuer, scope, number and validity — for anything we can document. Nothing is published as an endorsement until verified.

What is the lead time from sampling to bulk?

Lead time is confirmed against construction, colour count and the selected source. We give you a dated plan at quotation stage and track milestones in your order file.
