Poplin

Fine, close plain weave with a crisp, dry hand — the standard for shirting and fine T-shirts.

Request a Fabric MatchBrowse the Fabric Library
01

Overview

Poplin is a plain weave (one warp over, one weft under) run at a higher weft density than warp density, which gives the fabric its characteristic crispness, slight crosswise hand and clean surface. It is the default construction for dress shirts, fine T-shirts and uniform programs where a structured, smooth face is required.

We source poplin in mercerised 100% cotton for premium shirting, CVC and TC for cost-efficient programs, and fine polyester for uniform and industrial use. The key negotiation points are yarn count, mercerisation level, shrinkage control and colour fastness — all of which we specify before sampling and verify at both quality gates.

03

Construction

Plain 1/1 weave with finer weft than warp, typically 44/2–52/2 yarn counts. Mercerisation raises lustre, strength and dye affinity in cotton versions. Sanforisation or relaxed processing controls shrinkage to buyer tolerance.

04

Applications

Dress shirts

Mercerised cotton poplin with controlled shrinkage and fastness.

T-shirts & polos

Fine poplin and poplin-blend versions for structured knits and woven tees.

Uniforms

Polyester poplin for easy-care institutional programs.

08

Questions from the woven fabrics desk

What is the MOQ for woven fabric?

MOQ varies by construction, colour and source mill. Yarn-dyed and multi-colour programs typically carry higher minimums than solid colors. We confirm the exact MOQ when we return your mill-matched options.

Can you match a reference swatch?

Yes. Send a physical sample or high-resolution photos with target weight and width. We return strike-offs or equivalent mill options with a written specification comparison.

Do you stock woven fabric in Bangladesh?

We coordinate local distribution from Uttara, Dhaka. Stocked items are listed in the library; made-to-order items follow the sourcing process with confirmed lead times.

Which certifications apply to woven programs?

Certification depends on the mill and the program. We provide certificate metadata — issuer, scope, number and validity — for anything we can document. Nothing is published as an endorsement until verified.
